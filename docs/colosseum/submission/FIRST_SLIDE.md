# First Slide (title card for the pitch video and any deck)

> Rule: product name + one line of **at most 5 words**. The guide's slide rules apply:
> takeaway instead of a label, no superlatives, readable in under 15 seconds.

## Options

| # | Line under "Araf Protocol" | Words | Why / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (recommended)** | **Stablecoin-to-bank escrow. No moderator.** | 5 | Says what it is and what is different. A stranger can repeat it. Matches one-liner A in FORM.md. |
| 2 | Disputes cost both sides money. | 5 | States the mechanism, which is the real insight, but does not say what the product is. Works better as slide 3 ("Solution") title. |
| 3 | Trust time, not moderators. | 4 | Short version of the README tagline ("Trust the Time, Not the Oracle"). Memorable, but "oracle" and "time" both need explaining to a non-crypto judge. Good as the closing line. |

Word count rule used: hyphenated "Stablecoin-to-bank" counts as one word.
