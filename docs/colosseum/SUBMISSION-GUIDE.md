## Ecosystem Tracks (8 Total)

You can select up to **3 tracks** during registration. Every submission also competes for the general awards. Track prizes are additive to general prizes. (llms-full.txt:71-110)

### High-Prize Tracks ($100K each)

- **Solana:** 10 products × $10K = $100K
- **Tempo:** 10 products × $10K = $100K
- **Hyperliquid:** 10 products × $10K (Hypercore or HyperEVM)
- **Zcash:** 10 products × $10K (chain or asset)

### Lower-Prize Tracks ($25K each)

- **Ethereum L1:** 5 products × $5K = $25K
- **Base:** 5 products × $5K = $25K
- **Arbitrum:** 5 products × $5K = $25K
- **Robinhood Chain:** 5 products × $5K = $25K

**Important:** Colosseum's accelerator and venture fund still back Solana founders. If you build on another chain, you compete for general awards and your ecosystem track, but not for the accelerator. (llms-full.txt:73-75)

## What is Colosseum?

Colosseum is the largest startup competition in the Solana ecosystem and operates a pre-seed fund. For the first time, it's open to all blockchains, but the successful accelerator funding still backs Solana founders. Colosseum Org LLC operates it independently. (llms-full.txt:12)

**Key numbers:**
- **Prize pool:** $840,000 (general awards + 8 ecosystem tracks)
- **Venture fund:** $2.5M, Colosseum's own investment fund
- **Accelerator:** Selected teams receive $250K pre-seed + 12 weeks in San Francisco (first 2 weeks in-person, remaining 10 weeks remote-friendly) (llms-full.txt:90)
- **Türkiye pool:** 10,000 USDG (via Superteam Earn, in addition to Colosseum awards)
- **Past participation:** 9,000+ products submitted (across Colosseum's history)
- **Past investments:** 74 portfolio startups, $60M Fund I (llms-full.txt:59-62)

**Purpose:** This is not a hackathon; it's a pre-seed funnel. Judges score like startup investors; 5 of 7 criteria are business criteria. (llms-full.txt:55-87)

**Timeline:** From competition launch through deadline. Late registrations accepted until 12 October 23:59 PT.

Steps (llms-full.txt:183-192):
1. **Register via Superteam Türkiye referral link**

3. **Register officially for the competition** - Click the "Register" button (account ≠ registration)

4. **Set your location to Türkiye** - Required to compete in the Türkiye pool

5. **Register all team members** - Each member must register with their own account; missing members cause silent disqualification (llms-full.txt:247)

6. **Fill out the Superteam Türkiye orientation form**  
   https://forms.gle/EELv1S3eD1nEytrq7

7. **Find a team if needed:** Use Cofounder Matching or the Telegram group

**Team size:** Solo participation is allowed, but judges prefer teams of 3+ (llms-full.txt:191).

**Eligibility check:** The competition is for new startups that haven't raised significant outside capital. If you've raised funds previously, read the rules. (llms-full.txt:299)

### Phase 2: Idea Validation and Building (Weeks 1-3)

**Duration:** 14 September - 5 October (3 weeks)

#### Week 1: Narrowest Possible MVP (llms-full.txt:194-200)

**Goal:** One user, one job, one screen. Chain integration working on devnet.

Checklist:
- [ ] Cut scope: overly broad projects are the leading cause of failure
- [ ] Wallet connect + real transaction: chain integration early and real
- [ ] Use existing stack (no rewrites): Helius/Triton (RPC), Privy (auth), Jupiter (swaps), Squads (multisig), Pyth (prices)
- [ ] Post your first build-in-public update: day one is better than day twenty (llms-full.txt:200)

#### Week 2: Show it to People (llms-full.txt:202-208)

**Goal:** 10 real users tested it, and you have quotes.

Checklist:
- [ ] Public URL: GitHub link ≠ demo
- [ ] Show to 10 people matching your target customer profile, watch silently
- [ ] Document what broke. Fix blockers to adoption; ignore annoyances.
- [ ] Start pitch materials: Deck outline + story to tell on camera  
  **Structure:** Problem → Solution → Demo → Team (llms-full.txt:208)

#### Week 3: Traction and Narrative (llms-full.txt:210-217)

**Goal:** Numbers on a slide with a story explaining them.

Checklist:
- [ ] Instrument the product: users, retained users, transactions, volume. Pick two to defend to an investor.
- [ ] Build narrative around the numbers, not around vision.
- [ ] Record a rough pitch and watch at 1x speed. If hook isn't in first 10 seconds, rewrite it (llms-full.txt:215)
- [ ] Ask Superteam Türkiye to amplify a real milestone (result, not announcement)

## Submission Form Fields (Preparation)

**⚠️ Note:** Write all answers in a doc first, ensure they fit character limits, then paste. Writing answers for the first time in the form costs far more time. (llms-full.txt:592-598)

**Submit button:** Opens 6 October 4:00 PT. Drafts are saveable before then—start writing immediately. (llms-full.txt:221)

### Tier 1: Visible on Public Page (llms-full.txt:599-611)

Anyone with your link sees these. Write for a stranger, not for judges.

- **Project name** - Product name
- **Brief description** (max 500 char) - Your one-liner, unchanged
- **Project website** (optional)
- **Product category** - Choose closest fit if multiple apply
- **Team primary location** - **SELECT TÜRKIYE** (condition for Türkiye pool)
- **Logo/graphic** (JPEG/PNG/WEBP/GIF, max 20MB) - Before compression
- **GitHub link** - Direct repo link (not org/profile page). Can stay private if you grant access to `hackathon@colosseum.com`.
- **Pitch video** (max 2 min) - Your own voice and face; Loom or YouTube
- **X profile** - Verified account

### Tier 2: Shared with Team, Judges and Colosseum (llms-full.txt:613-627)

Not public, but every judge reads these first.

- **What are you building, and who is it for?** (max 1000 char)
- **Why did you decide to build this, and why now?** (max 1000 char)
- **What technologies and AI tools are you using?** - Include developer tools alongside AI tools
- **Which chains are you using?** - Only ones actually integrated, not claimed
- **How does your product use these chains?** (max 500 char) - **FORCED integrations get caught here. If your product exists without a chain, you must justify deeply.**
- **Is this a mobile-focused dApp?** (Yes/No)
- **Team Telegram contact** - Prize distribution and accelerator interviews happen here; assign someone who monitors it
- **Did anyone not on the team do meaningful work?** (max 600 char, answer honestly)
- **Anything else judges should know?** (max 500 char, optional but often helpful)
- **Demo video** (max 3 min, separate from pitch) - Live product; not slides, not code walkthrough
- **Live product link** + access instructions (if required)

### Tier 3: Accelerator Screening (Private, llms-full.txt:629-640)

Only Colosseum and organizers see these. Required even if you don't want the accelerator; this is how Colosseum decides who to consider.

- **How do you know people actually need, or will need, this product?** (max 1000 char)
- **How far along are you? Do you have users?** Be as specific as possible. (max 1000 char)
- **Who else is building in this space, and what do you think they're getting wrong?** (max 1000 char)
- **How do you make money, or plan to?** (max 500 char)
- **How long has each of you been working on this? Full-time?** (max 500 char)
- **Where is each team member based, and do you work together in person?** Will that change after funding? (max 500 char)
- **Yes/No:** Have you formed a legal entity (company)?
- **Yes/No:** Have you taken investment?
- **Yes/No:** Are you currently fundraising?
- **Yes/No:** Do you have a live token?

**Note:** "No" answers are fine. Thin open-ended answers read worse than early-stage honesty. Accelerator screening rewards simple, direct answers.

## Disqualification Rules (2 Total)

**These are not style points. Two teams have already been removed from competitions after doing the work.** (llms-full.txt:537-548)

### 1. One Product Per Team, One Per Person

- **A team may submit only one product**
- **A person may appear on only one submission**
- You cannot submit two projects
- You cannot appear on two submissions

### 2. Disclosure of Prior Code (Mandatory)

- You may start before the window opens and reuse your own code
- **BUT** only work done inside the window is judged
- **You MUST disclose all prior development on the submission form**

**Penalty for hiding:** Disqualification + ban from future hackathons + prize revocation

**Penalty for disclosing:** None. Teams with a running start have placed well. Disclosure is not a risk.

**Note:** Using other people's open source is allowed and encouraged. This rule is only about your own prior code. (llms-full.txt:547)

## Judging Criteria

### Official 7 Criteria (llms-full.txt:263-279)

1. **Founder-market fit and motivation** - Why you, specifically, for this problem?
2. **Unique insight and technology** - What do you know that the room doesn't?
3. **Product execution quality** - Does it work, and does it feel built?
4. **Market size** - Is the ceiling worth an investor's time?
5. **Founder communication** - Can you explain it in two minutes?
6. **Business viability** - How does it make money?
7. **Traction and demand** - Evidence someone wants it?

**Remember:** Five are business criteria. This is a startup competition in hackathon clothing. (llms-full.txt:281)

### 6 Criteria Listed in Official Rules (llms-full.txt:288-298)

- Functionality
- Potential impact
- Novelty
- UX
- Open-source and composability
- Business plan

**The conflict:** Code quality. FAQ says they don't grade style/best practices. Rules say code quality is a criterion. Resolution: something must work, and judges can see you built it sensibly. Ship functional, then stop. Extra refactoring buys nothing. (llms-full.txt:291-297)

### AI Pre-Filtering (llms-full.txt:282-286)

Before humans, an internal AI engine filters and flags. It evaluates:
- Founder-market fit
- Product velocity
- User demand
- Market timing
- Evidence of grit (track record)

**Implication:** Track record matters; you can't build it in four weeks. Enter with your existing track record, documented and linkable, not a clean slate.

**Panel stage:** 15-minute call (not a new demo). Asked: "What did you ship last week?" "Are you full-time?"

## Pitch and Demo Video Tactics

### Video > Deck (llms-full.txt:506-510)

Superteam Balkan analyzed 139 winning submissions:
- **88% video:** 63 Loom, 49 YouTube, 8 Vimeo
- **12% PDF/slide:** ~16 decks

Grand Champion: YouTube video.

**Why:** Judges review 100+ submissions solo. Video does the job a deck would. (llms-full.txt:510)

### First 10 Seconds Decide (llms-full.txt:305)

Open with problem + solution, not your origin story. Judges review hundreds. (llms-full.txt:305)

### Structure: Problem → Solution → Demo → Team (llms-full.txt:308)

Deviating costs clarity and buys nothing. Write business model in the form, not the video (padding). (llms-full.txt:309)

### The One-Liner is Powerful (llms-full.txt:514-519)

A strong one-liner gets you shortlisted on its own.
- Judge remembers it an hour later.
- Investor repeats it to partners when you're gone.
- Example: "Stablecoin neobank for SMBs" (over revolutionary nonsense)

**Five-slide rules:**
1. Takeaway in the title - "Users lose $4M/year to wallet drains" not "Problem"
2. One point per slide
3. Under 40 words; past that, readers skim
4. Cut superlatives; prove it: "15 seconds start-to-finish", "$400/user/month savings"
5. Every slide readable in 15 seconds; ~90 seconds total

(llms-full.txt:527-538)

### Four Ways to Lose After Doing the Work (llms-full.txt:527-538)

- **Overselling the future:** Grand vision, no track record = pitch, not product. Show the MVP.
- **Forced crypto integration:** If product works without a chain, justify use deeply or don't claim it.
- **Weak demo:** GitHub link ≠ demo. Judge needs something working to watch.
- **Missing permissions:** Private repo/Google Doc judge can't open. **Most common fatal mistake.** If private, grant `hackathon@colosseum.com` access and have an outsider test every link.

(llms-full.txt:531-538)

## Tips for Making Pitch and Demo Videos

### Pre-Production (llms-full.txt:621-627)

1. Write each answer in a doc first, check character limits - don't write live in form
2. Have someone outside the team test pitch video + demo video + GitHub link (accessibility check)
3. Decide your chain list before answering "how does your product use these chains"
4. Answer Tier 3 (accelerator screen) even if you don't want the accelerator

### Pitch Video Content (llms-full.txt:499)

Max 2 minutes. Explain *why*: who, what, why you started, market, customer source, working demo.

### Demo Video Content (llms-full.txt:500)

Max 3 minutes. Technical *how*. Not slides, not code walkthrough. Live product judges can watch.

## After the Deadline: Judging and Beyond

### Judging Process (llms-full.txt:555-567)

- **Deadline:** 12 October 23:59 PT
- **Results:** Around 5 December (7 weeks)
- **Momentum period:** 2 weeks after deadline, judges and investors watch. Prize hunters stop; builders continue.
- **Panel call:** Shortlisted teams get 15-minute calls. Not a new demo; "what did you ship last week?" and "full-time?" assess velocity and commitment. (llms-full.txt:285-286)

### First-Time Wins Are Rare (llms-full.txt:563-567)

- Unruggable entered 4 Colosseum hackathons: honor mention → track win → Grand Championship
- **Top group placement = accelerator eligible** - Not just Grand Champion, but top 20 feed into accelerator
- Colosseum's Hall of Fame lists past winners on their site

### Accelerator Paths (llms-full.txt:569-575)

**Colosseum Accelerator (Only via hackathon)**
- $250K pre-seed (founder-friendly terms)
- 12-week program (first 2 weeks San Francisco, remaining 10 remote-friendly)

**Other Accelerators**
- Alliance DAO: $500K (~7% equity)
- Orange DAO: $100K (MFN SAFE)
- Monke Foundry
- Colosseum Eternal: $25K USDC (self-paced 4-week sprint, currently paused, expected back around November)

### Grant Paths (llms-full.txt:577-584)

## Pre-Submission Checklist

Before uploading, review your submission:

### Idea and Validation
- [ ] One-liner written (max one clear sentence)
- [ ] Market problem validated with 10 real people
- [ ] Gap-check done with Colosseum Copilot
- [ ] Business model = 3 sentences, fits 500 characters (plan form field)

### Product
- [ ] MVP live on public domain (not localhost screenshot)
- [ ] Wallet connect + real transaction shipped
- [ ] UI simple and logical (extra features later)
- [ ] Product truly requires chain integration in spec (not forced)
- [ ] Repo public or access granted to `hackathon@colosseum.com`
- [ ] README/docs clear to someone outside team

### Track Record / Social
- [ ] X account verified
- [ ] Posting daily (minimum 5x/week last 4 weeks)
- [ ] Following judge panel (5 Colosseum + 15 track)
- [ ] 1-2 milestones requested amplification from Superteam Türkiye

### Videos and Materials

- [ ] Demo video (max 3 min): Technical, product working live, not slides, not code walkthrough
- [ ] Logo/graphic (JPEG/PNG/WEBP/GIF, max 20MB)
- [ ] Videos tested with someone outside team can watch them
- [ ] Pitch video uploaded to correct location (public project page)

### Form Readiness
